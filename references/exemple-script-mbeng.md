# Video ad with voiceover and character (draft for approval) — Mbeng example

The original French script of the Mbeng ad, kept as a real example: the voiceover lines stay in French
(as produced); the rest is translated.

Formats: 9:16 (TikTok, Reels, WhatsApp Status) and 16:9 (YouTube). Target length: 35 to 40 s.
Character: a Cameroonian student, drawn in SVG in Remotion, who shows the app
on their phone (real screenshots). Voice: ElevenLabs (`eleven_multilingual_v2`), in French.
Every number quoted comes from `src/content/facts.ts` (data id in brackets).
